# The Geotic Realm

## What the Geotic Realm Is

The **Geotic Realm** defines the class of **maxanthropic** worlds within the **Terric Domain**:  
COH-amegic environments whose physical structure **naturally supports stable, long-term human habitation** without extensive technological mediation.

Geotic worlds are not required to be perfect Earth analogs.  
They are required to be **structurally forgiving**.

A Geotic world supports:

- COH-animosys robustly and persistently    
- open-air human habitation with minimal intervention    
- long-term climatic and geochemical stability    

In short:

> **Geotic worlds are those where humans can live, not merely survive.**
## Axis Placement (Explicit and Non-Negotiable)

The Geotic Realm is defined by the **intersection** of two axes:

- **Amegic Axis:** COH-amegic (inherited from Biotic)    
- **Anthropic Axis:** maxanthropic    

It further refines, but does not replace, the Terric Domain.

- **Morphotic Axis:** typically duromonic, but not defined here    

This matters.  The Geotic Realm does **not** describe:

- cultural suitability   
- aesthetic similarity to Earth    
- biological inevitability    
- narrative importance    

Those are downstream concerns.
## What “Geotic” Means (and What It Doesn’t)

**Geotic means “naturally habitable for humans.”** It does _not_ mean:

- identical to Earth    
- climatically uniform    
- ecologically mature    
- free of hazards    

A Geotic world may be:

- colder or warmer than Earth    
- wetter or drier    
- biologically young    
- sparsely populated    

Yet still be Geotic if its **baseline physical regime** supports human life without extraordinary engineering.

> **Geotic is about habitability, not perfection.**
## Geotic as the Earthlike Core of Terric Space

Within the Terric Domain, the Geotic Realm occupies the **structural core**:  
the region where planetary mass, gravity, atmosphere, and heat flow **align naturally** with human physiology and COH-animosys.

This is the zone where:

- breathable atmospheres are stable    
- hydrospheres persist over geological time    
- plate tectonics and magnetic shielding are common    
- climates self-regulate within tolerable bounds    

Geotic worlds are therefore **evolution-friendly**, not merely survivable.
## Relation to Other Animotic Classes

The Geotic Realm is a **subset** of the Terric Domain and a **superset** of the Gaean Province.

- **Terric Domain** — human survival possible    
- **Geotic Realm** — human habitation viable    
- **Gaean Province** — human comfort native    

All Gaeans are Geotic.  
Most Geotics are not Gaean.

This asymmetry is deliberate.
## Geotic vs. Gaean (Critical Distinction)

The Geotic Realm is defined by **structural habitability**.  
The **Gaean Province** is defined by **anthropic optimality**.

Geotic worlds:

- support human life naturally    
- tolerate variation in gravity, climate, and chemistry    
- permit ecological diversity    

Gaean worlds:

- cluster tightly around Earth-normal parameters    
- minimize physiological and environmental stress    
- occupy the deepest anthropic sweet spot    

Gaeans are rare by construction.  
Geotics are rare by circumstance.
## Time Matters: Geotic Is a State, Not a Guarantee

Like all animotic classifications, Geotic is **state-based**.

A world may:

- enter the Geotic Realm as atmospheres stabilize    
- leave it as climates drift or gravity changes    
- pass through Geotic briefly on the way to Gaean    
- degrade back to Terric through stellar or geological change    

Geotic status reflects **present structure**, not destiny.
## Why the Geotic Realm Exists in Wimby

Worldbuilders need a way to say:

> “Humans can live here, and the planet will mostly cooperate.”

The Geotic Realm provides that answer without:

- assuming Earth identity    
- collapsing habitability into comfort    
- treating humans as cosmically typical    

It marks the **true corridor of habitability** within the Biotic Order:  
broad enough for diversity, narrow enough to mean something.
## Relationship to Planemonics (Explicit but Secondary)

The Geotic Realm is **not defined by numerical thresholds**.

Certain ranges of:

- mass    
- gravity    
- density    
- escape velocity    

**correlate strongly** with Geotic habitability, while others exclude it outright. These correlations are treated as **design guidance**, not classification criteria.

> Geotic defines _natural habitability_.  
> Planemonics constrains _where that habitability is likely_.

The arrow only points one way.
## Summary (for Fast Readers)

- **Geotic = maxanthropic Terric worlds**    
- COH-animosys thrives, and humans can live openly    
- Geotic does not imply Earth identity or perfection    
- It is the Earthlike core of Terric space    
- All Gaeans are Geotic; most Geotics are not Gaean    
- Geotic is state-based and historically contingent    

If a world is Geotic, human life is naturally supported.  
How _pleasant_ that life is depends on whether it is also Gaean.
