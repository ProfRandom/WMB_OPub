# Carboxyhydroxyl Animosys (COH "Life")

## What “COH” Actually Means

**COH-animosys** refers to any form of biology whose fundamental chemistry is built around:
- **C**arbon    
- **O**xygen    
- **H**ydrogen    

These three elements form the structural, energetic, and metabolic backbone of what we think of as “Earthlike” animosys — but the COH definition is _not limited to Terran microbiology_. It is a **generalized biochemical family** defined by:

- **Carbon** as the primary structural element    
- **Oxygen** as the dominant electron acceptor and bond partner    
- **Hydrogen** as both solvent component and reductant
    
This triad produces the **carboxyhydroxyl** chemical regime: a suite of organic molecules with carboxyl, hydroxyl, carbonyl, and related functional groups.
## Why COH and not CHNOPS?
Because CHNOPS is a **list of common elements**, not a _chemical identity class_.

**COH**, by contrast, is:
- thermodynamically meaningful    
- chemically predictive    
- structurally diagnostic    
- habitability-relevant    
- cross-planet applicable    

COH-animosys describes a _phase of biochemistry_, not a periodic-table checklist.

If a world can sustain **liquid-phase water**, **organic carbon chains**, and **oxygen-mediated energy cycling**, it is COH-animosys compatible.
## Key Requirements of COH-animosys

A COH animosphere depends on:
### 1. Liquid-phase solvents
- Usually water, but COH-animosys may tolerate supercritical or brine phases.
### 2. Oxidation gradients
- Animosys must access usable redox pairs — oxygen is the star electron acceptor, but not always the only one.
### 3. Organic carbon chains
- Complex macromolecules built from C, O, H — the basis for:
	- amino acids    
	- sugars    
	- lipids    
	- nucleic analogs
### 4. Stable surface or subsurface environments
- Temperature and pressure must permit:
	- liquid water    
	- flexible organic chemistry    
	- non-degenerate molecular structures
### 5. Planetary-scale recycling
- COH-animosys thrives on cycling:
	- carbon    
	- oxygen    
	- hydrogen    
	- water    
	- nutrients
— through atmosphere, hydrosphere, or lithosphere.

These conditions are rare cosmically — hence the steep attrition from Biotic to Terric to Geotic to Gaean environments.
## What COH-animosys Is _Not_

To avoid confusion:
### ❌ Not “Earth clone animosys.”
- The COH framework allows for:
	- different genetic systems    
	- different amino acids    
	- different polymer backbones    
	- different metabolic pathways    
	- wildly different ecosystems
— but all operating in the _same biochemical envelope_.
### ❌ Not dependent on nitrogen, phosphorus, sulfur, or trace metals.
- They may be present — often are — but COH-animosys can exist across a variety of elemental ecosystems.
### ❌ Not limited to oxygenated atmospheres.
- Anaerobic COH-animosys dominated Earth for ~2.4 billion years.
### ❌ Not tied to human habitability.
- Humans are an extremely narrow COH subcase.  
	- COH-animosys as a whole is far broader.
