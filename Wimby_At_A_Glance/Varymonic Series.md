# The Varymonic  Mass Series

All physical bodies (_monons_) in the WMB framework are described along two orthogonal axes:
- their **Varymonic Series**, which defines the degree of _gravity-dominated_ self-coherence, and
- their **Morphological Character**, which defines material composition and structure.

Monons first divide into **Synomons** (cohesion-bound) and **Varymons** (gravity-bound).

Only _varymons_ admit Varymonic Series distinctions, because these classes arise from **gravitational self-organization**, **compression physics**, and **fusion thresholds**.

At the broadest gravitational scale, all **varymons** fall into one of four Varymonic Series:
- **Duromons** — all varymons with masses below the degenerate matter threshold ($< 5$ j).  
- **Mesomons** — all varymons with masses above $≥ 5$ j and < $80$ j ($0.08$ s)
- **Fusomons** — all varymons with masses in the stable hydrogen-fusion range ($0.08 ·· 300$ s).  
    Subdivided by the **stellar spectral-class system**, which defines their thermodynamic and photometric behavior.    
- **Peromons** — all varymons whose masses exceed the upper stellar-stability limit ($> 300$ s).  
    These occupy the **trans-stellar regime** of radiative instability, pair-creation collapse, or exotic compact states.
## **Varymonic Axis**

_Defines gravitational scale and self-coherence — how strongly a body’s gravity shapes its structure._

- Applies **only to varymons**    
- Ranges from micromons to hypermons    
- Reflects internal pressure, compression, fusion onset, and relativistic collapse    
- Field: **Mononics**
## **Morphotic Axis

_Defines material/structural composition — what the body is made of._

This is covered in a separate series of articles.
# 2. Varymon Series

_All gravitationally-coherent monons_

This superclass contains four major subclasses, distinguished by the dominant internal physics shaping each regime:

- **Duromons** — classical-matter varymons  
- **Mesomons** — partially degenerate varymons  
- **Fusomons** — fusion-regime varymons  
- **Peromons** — relativistic/exotic varymons
## 2.1. Duromons
### _Matter-regime monons ($0.0018$ t → $1590$ t)_

Bodies whose structure is dominated by **classical matter physics**:
- density    
- composition    
- radius    
- phase state    
- hydrostatic self-cohesion    
- escape velocity    
- non-fusion thermodynamics
    
**Duromons are where Animotics most commonly manifest**, because only classical-matter regimes support stable chemical and ecological substrates.
## 2.2. Mesomons ($1590$ t → $4131$ t; $5$ j → $13$ j)

This superclass includes:
- transimons
	- electron-degenerate bodies with constant radius
- intermons    
	- deuterium-fusors    
	- non-hydrogenic fusors
	    - the entire L–T–Y dwarf continuum
    
Mesomons are the “liminal monons,” bridging duromons and fusomons.
### _Transimon ($1590$ t → $4131$ t; $5$ j → $13$ j)_

Bodies bridging planetary and brown-dwarf physics, dominated by:
- electron degeneracy pressure,    
- deep convective envelopes,    
### _Intermons ($13$ j → $80$ j)_

Bodies bridging planetary and stellar physics, dominated by:
- partial fusion,    
- deep convective envelopes,    
- brown-dwarf thermodynamics,    
- and non-hydrogen catalytic fusion episodes.
    
They “borrow rules” from both duromonic and fusomonic regimes.
## 2.3. Fusomons
### _Stellar monons ($80$ j → ~$300$ s)_

Bodies defined by **sustained core hydrogen fusion**, including:
- main-sequence stars    
- pre-/post-main-sequence fusors    
- spectral-class morphotypes    
- luminosity-cluster somatypes    
- variability families    

The fusomon system already has:
- spectral classes    
- luminosity classes    
- ontozones    
- perannual & nucleal orbit mapping    
- Solar Analogs/Cognates/Twins
    
This is the most "borrowed-in" subsystem in WMB (with some tweaks).
## 2.4. Peromons
### _Ultra-/Supermassive monons (> $10$ megaterran)_

Bodies whose structure is dominated by:
- relativity    
- spacetime curvature    
- degenerate pressure    
- accretion physics    
- relativistic mass aggregation    
- event-horizon dynamics    

Including:
- supramons (stellar-mass black holes and neutron stars)    
- ultramons supermassive black holes (SMBHs)    
- hypermons (hypercollapses, primordial monsters, etc.)    

Peromons complete the varymonic mass spectrum: the final extreme for independently self-coherent bodies.
