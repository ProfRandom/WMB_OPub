# COH-animosys and the Animotic Classes
Animotics classifies worlds by their _physical eligibility_ to support COH-animosys.  
That hierarchy looks like this:
### Biotic:
- The world is structurally capable of COH biochemistry.
### Terric:
- The world is structurally capable of COH biochemistry _in a human-adjacent envelope_ (parahabitable).
### Geotic:
- The world is physically able to support open-air human habitation.
### Gaean:
- The world comfortably supports human-normal ecosystems and biology.
### Kybelic:
- Superhabitable environments where COH-animosys thrives at maximal complexity, density, and metabolic richness.

Crucially:
> **Animotic classes describe physical potential, not actualized biospheres.** COH-animosys may or may not be present on a given Biotic, Terric, or even Geotic world.

Environmental realization is governed by **Milieutics**, not Animotics.
