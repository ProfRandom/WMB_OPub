# WMB Terminology: “Bio-” vs. “Animo-”

In WMB, the prefix **bio-** applies _only_ to **COH-amegic animosys** — carbon–oxygen–hydrogen biochemistry operating within the carboxyhydroxyl regime. Terms such as _biochemistry_, _biota_, _biosphere_, and _biogenic processes_ refer specifically to COH-animosys and COH-ecosystems.

An **animegic** system is one that can support or give rise to animosys of _any_ animochemical lineage, not only COH-animosys.

Common animo-terms include:
- **Animegic** — capable of supporting or generating animotes, regardless of chemical family    
- **Animotics** — study of animegic potential and animosys-systems broadly    
- **Animogenesis** — processes capable of producing animotes of any lineage    
- **Animozones** — spatial or thermodynamic regions where animosys of any chemistry may emerge or persist    
- **Animogy** — energy and metabolic principles general to all forms of animosys    
- **Animotes** — individual animosys-forms of any chemical lineage    
- **Animota** — ecological assemblies or communities of animotes (the analogue of “biota”)    
- **Animosustents** — substrates, gradients, and resources that sustain animotes of any chemistry
    
When a more specific non-COH animosys family is defined, WMB names it with its own compositional prefix (e.g., **ferro-** for iron-redox animosys, **silico-** for silicon-chain biochemistry, **azo-** for nitrogen-redox organisms, etc.).

Thus:

> **COH-animosys is the only form of animosys entitled to the “bio-” prefix. All others fall under the broader class of animo-animosys unless a more specific lineage prefix is formally defined.**
