# The Terric Domain

## What the Terric Domain Is

The **Terric Domain** defines the class of **mesanthropic** worlds within the **Biotic Order**:  
COH-amegic environments whose physical structure is **potentially survivable for humans**, given moderate technological support.

Terric worlds are not required to be Earthlike.  
They are required to be **human-adjacent**.

A Terric world supports:

- COH-animosys in principle    
- long-term surface or subsurface stability    
- gravity, pressure, and chemistry within broad human tolerances    

In short:

> **Terric worlds are those where humans can plausibly survive without rewriting their biology.**

## Axis Placement (Explicit and Non-Negotiable)

The Terric Domain is defined by the **intersection** of two axes:

- **Amegic Axis:** COH-amegic (inherited from Biotic)    
- **Anthropic Axis:** mesanthropic    

It does **not** introduce a new substrate, nor does it define morphology.

- **Morphotic Axis:** typically duromonic, but not defined here    

This matters.  The Terric Domain does **not** describe:

- ecological richness    
- climate stability    
- biospheric maturity    
- comfort or convenience    

Those are downstream refinements.
## What “Terric” Means (and What It Doesn’t)

**Terric means “structurally compatible with human survival.”**

It does _not_ mean:

- breathable without assistance    
- temperate    
- biologically rich    
- politically or narratively convenient    

A Terric world may be:

- hostile at the surface    
- thin-aired or dense-aired    
- geologically young or unstable    
- biologically sparse or sterile    

Yet still remain Terric if its **physical envelope** permits sustained human presence with engineering rather than transformation.

> **Terric is about survivability, not comfort.**
## Terric as the Human-Adjacent Envelope

Within the Biotic Order, the Terric Domain represents the **widest physically meaningful range** of worlds that humans can interact with directly.

This is the zone where:

- pressure vessels suffice    
- habitats are plausible    
- terraforming is meaningful    
- long-term colonization is imaginable    

Below Terric, human presence becomes transient or impossible.  
Above Terric, additional constraints narrow the field.

Terric is therefore the **workhorse category** of human-facing worldbuilding.
## Relation to Other Animotic Classes

The Terric Domain is a **subset** of the Biotic Order and a **superset** of more restrictive anthropic classes.

- **Biotic Order** — COH-animosys possible    
- **Terric Domain** — human survival possible    
- **Geotic Realm** — human habitation broadly viable    
- **Gaean Province** — human comfort native    

All Geotics and Gaeans are Terric.  
Most Terrics are neither.

This asymmetry is intentional.
## Terric vs. Kybelic (Critical Distinction)

The Terric Domain is **anthropic**.  
The **Kybelic Domain** is **amegic**.

Kybelic worlds:

- are optimized for COH-animosys productivity    
- often exceed human gravity, pressure, or metabolic tolerance    
- may host extraordinarily rich biospheres    

Terric worlds:

- prioritize human compatibility    
- may sacrifice biospheric density for survivability    

Kybelics are **animotically lateral**, not anthropically nested.
## Time Matters: Terric Is a State, Not a Destiny

Like all animotic classifications, Terric is **state-based**.

A world may:

- enter the Terric Domain as atmospheres thicken    
- leave it as gravity increases or volatiles are lost    
- pass through Terric briefly on the way to Geotic    
- fall back out due to climate collapse or stellar evolution    

Terric is a **window**, not a guarantee.
## Why the Terric Domain Exists in Wimby

Worldbuilders need a way to say:

> “Humans can live here — but it won’t be easy.”

The Terric Domain provides that answer without:

- conflating survivability with habitability   
- collapsing engineering into biology    
- assuming Earth norms by default    

It is the **minimum anthropic threshold** above Biotic worlds and the **maximum design freedom** below Geotic ones.
## Relationship to Planemonics (Explicit but Secondary)

The Terric Domain is **not defined by equations**.

Certain ranges of:

- mass    
- gravity    
- density    
- escape velocity    

**correlate strongly** with Terric survivability, while others exclude it outright. These relationships are discussed where they belong: in **planemonic guidance**, not animotic definition.

> Terric defines _who can survive_.  
> Planemonics constrains _where that survival is plausible_.

The arrow only points one way.

## Summary (for Fast Readers)

- **Terric = mesanthropic Biotic worlds**    
- COH-animosys is possible, and humans can survive with support    
- Terric does not imply comfort, stability, or richness    
- It is the broadest human-adjacent envelope    
- All Geotics and Gaeans are Terric; most Terrics are neither    
- Kybelics are lateral, not nested    
- Terric is state-based and historically contingent    

If a world is Terric, humans can plausibly live there.  
Whether they _want_ to is another question entirely.
