# The Gaean Province

## What the Gaean Province Is

The **Gaean Province** defines the class of **melanthropic** worlds within the **Geotic Realm**:  
COH-amegic environments whose physical structure lies at the **center of human physiological, biomechanical, and ecological tolerance**.

Where Geotic worlds are broadly habitable, **Gaean worlds are natively comfortable**.

A Gaean world supports:

- open-air human life without assistance    
- effortless locomotion and respiration    
- long-term ecological stability    
- intuitive environmental interaction    

In short:

> **Gaean worlds are those where humans belong without modification.**
## Axis Placement (Explicit and Non-Negotiable)

The Gaean Province is defined by the **intersection** of two axes:

- **Amegic Axis:** COH-amegic (inherited from Biotic)    
- **Anthropic Axis:** melanthropic    

It is a **refinement**, not a redefinition, of the Geotic Realm.

- **Morphotic Axis:** typically duromonic, but not defined here    

This matters.  The Gaean Province does **not** describe:

- cultural familiarity    
- aesthetic similarity to Earth    
- biological inevitability    
- narrative importance    

It describes **anthropic optimality**.
## What “Gaean” Means (and What It Doesn’t)

**Gaean means “anthropically optimal.”**  It does _not_ mean:

- identical to Earth    
- ecologically static    
- biologically conservative    
- free of danger    

A Gaean world may be:

- alien in flora and fauna    
- climatically varied    
- tectonically active    
- culturally unfamiliar    

Yet still be Gaean if its **baseline physical regime** aligns closely with human norms.

> **Gaean is about ease, not sameness.**
## The Anthropically Effortless World

Within the Geotic Realm, the Gaean Province occupies the **deepest anthropic sweet spot**:  
the region where planetary structure minimizes physiological strain and environmental mediation.

This is the zone where:

- breathing is unconscious    
- walking feels natural    
- gravity disappears from awareness    
- weather is challenging but not hostile    
- ecosystems feel intuitively legible    

Gaean worlds do not merely permit human life.  
They **welcome it**.
## Relation to Other Animotic Classes

The Gaean Province is a **subset** of the Geotic Realm and the **most restrictive anthropic class** in the Biotic Order.

- **Terric Domain** — human survival possible    
- **Geotic Realm** — human habitation viable    
- **Gaean Province** — human comfort native    

All Gaeans are Geotic.  
Most Geotics are not Gaean.

This asymmetry is intentional and unavoidable.
## Rarity Is the Point

The Gaean Province is **narrow by definition**.

Only a fraction of Geotic worlds fall deeply enough into the anthropic optimum to qualify as Gaean. This is not a failure of generosity; it is a reflection of biological specificity.

Human physiology is:

- gravity-sensitive    
- pressure-sensitive    
- chemically constrained    
- evolutionarily contingent    

The fact that Gaeans are rare is not surprising.  
It is expected.

> **A world finely tuned to one lineage should be uncommon in a vast cosmos.**
## Time Matters: Gaean Is a Moment, Not a Birthright

Like all animotic classifications, Gaean is **state-based**.

A world may:

- pass briefly through the Gaean Province    
- remain Gaean for billions of years    
- drift out due to climatic or tectonic change    
- never enter it at all    

Gaean status reflects **current anthropic alignment**, not destiny or narrative privilege.
## Why the Gaean Province Exists in Wimby

Worldbuilders need a way to say:

> “This planet feels like home.”

The Gaean Province provides that answer without:

- declaring Earth uniqueness    
- collapsing comfort into habitability    
- pretending humans are universal    

It names the **rare convergence** of conditions under which human life is not merely possible, but **effortless**.
## Relationship to Planemonics (Explicit but Secondary)

The Gaean Province is **not defined by equations**.

Certain combinations of:

- mass    
- gravity    
- density    
- escape velocity    

**correlate strongly** with anthropic ease, while others impose subtle but persistent strain. These correlations are treated as **design guidance**, not definitional boundaries.

> Gaean defines _how a world feels to humans_.  
> Planemonics constrains _where that feeling is likely to occur_.

The arrow only points one way.

## Summary (for Fast Readers)

- **Gaean = melanthropic Geotic worlds**    
- Humans can live openly and effortlessly    
- Gaean does not imply Earth identity    
- It is the deepest anthropic sweet spot    
- All Gaeans are Geotic; most Geotics are not Gaean    
- Gaean is state-based and historically contingent    

If a world is Gaean, humans do not have to adapt.  
They simply arrive.
