# The Canonical Animotics Tree

**The Animotics Tree** is the canonical structural schema of the Worldmaking Basics animotics framework.

It enumerates representative **Orders, Domains, Realms, and Provinces** by which animosys-supporting environments are classified, and encodes their **nesting and lateral relationships**.

This document is a **reference artifact**, not an explanatory essay.  Conceptual definitions, phenomenology, and design guidance are provided in the linked articles for each category.

The Tree is **illustrative rather than exhaustive**: inclusion denotes ontological recognition, not completeness, prevalence, or developmental priority.
