 # Standard Constants
 - $c$ ⟶ speed of light ⟶ $2.9979 \times 10^8$ m/s
 -  $G$ ⟶ gravitational constant    
    - $6.67430 \times 10^{-11}$ m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻²        
- **$\sigma$** ⟶ Stefan–Boltzmann constant    
    - $5.670374419 \times 10^{-8}$ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴        
- **$h$** ⟶ Planck’s constant    
    - $6.62607015 \times 10^{-34}$ J·s        
- **$k_B$** ⟶ Boltzmann constant    
    - $1.380649 \times 10^{-23}$ J·K⁻¹
 - $AU$ ⟶ average Earth semi-major axis
	 - $1.496 \times 10^6$ km
	 - $1.496 \times 10^8$ m
 - $ly$ ⟶ light-year
	 - $9.461 \times 10^{12}$ km
	 - $63241.1$ AU
	 - $0.3066\; pc$
 - $pc$ ⟶ parsec
	 - $3.26156$ ly
 - $r♁$ ⟶ one Earth radius
	 - 6371 km
	 - $0.0092$ R⊙
 - $m♁$ ⟶ one Earth mass
	 - $5.972 \times 10^{24}$ kg
	 - $81.22\; m☽$
 - $m☽$ ⟶ one lunar mass
	 - $7.35 \times 10^{22}$ kg
	 - $0.0123$ m♁
- $☽_S$ ⟶ synodic month    
    - $29.53059^d$        
- $d_S$ ⟶ sidereal day    
    - $23^h 56^m 4.0905^s$
	    - $23.934469^h$
- $d⊙$ ⟶ solar day
    - $24^h$ (by definition)
    - $1440^m$
    - $86400^s$
    - > when the superscript ${}^d$ is used, _solar day_ is the base unit
 - $R⊙$ ⟶ one solar radius
	 - $6.9634 \times 10^5$ km
	 - 109 r♁
	 - $0.00465047$ AU
	 - $9.73116$ r♃
 - $M⊙$ ⟶ mass of the Sun
	 - $1.989 \times 10^{30}$ kg
	 - $3.33 \times 10^5$ m♁
	 - $1047.52$ M♃
 - $Y_S$ ⟶ 1 sidereal year
	 - $365.256363004^d$
	 - $31,558,149.54^s$
 - $Y_T$ ⟶ 1 tropical year
	 - $365.24219^d$
 - $Y_G$ ⟶ 1 Gregorian year
	 - $365.2425^d$
 - $☽ \alpha$ ⟶ average distance from Earth to the Moon
	 - $2.389 \times 10^5$ km
	 - $37.498$ r♁
 - $m♃$ ⟶ 1 Jupiter mass
	 - $1.898 \times 10^{27}$ kg
	 - $317.8$ m♁
	 - $9.5459 \times 10^{-4}$ M⊙
 - r♃ ⟶ 1 Jupiter radius
	 - $6.9911 \times 10^4$ km
	 - $11.2$ r♁
	 - $0.102763$ R⊙
