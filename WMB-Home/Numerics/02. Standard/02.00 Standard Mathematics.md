 # Standard Mathematics

This section covers several standard math equations and constants for ease of reference.

## 1. Property Assignment

$$
\begin{array}{c c c}
\text{m} & = & 0.0123\oplus \\
\text{property/parameter} & \text{relation} & \text{scalar + referent} & \\
\text{receptant} & & \text{assignant}
\end{array}
$$

- Receptant ⟶ that which is receiving the action of the relation
	- In this case, the symbol of the property/parameter
- Assignant ⟶ that which is assigning the action of the relation
	- In this case, the absolute value being related and the referent element donating the value

## 2. Property Relationship

$$
\begin{array}{c c c}
\text{1}\text{♃} & = & 317.8\oplus \\
\text{scalar + referent} & \text{relation} & \text{scalar + referent} \\
\hspace{0.25em}\text{receptant} & & \text{assignant}
\end{array}
$$

- Receptant ⟶ that which is receiving the action of the relation
	- In this case, the relative value being related and the symbol of the element receiving the value
- Assignant ⟶ that which is assigning the action of the relation
	- In this case, the absolute value being related and the referent element donating the value
